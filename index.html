01 / ABOUT

The person behind the projects.

A more personal look at how DEVZIKKY TECH came together.

Who I am

I’m DEVZIKKY, a developer and builder based in Lagos, Nigeria. DEVZIKKY TECH is where I bring together the things I experiment with: websites, APIs, AI assistants, email tools, bots, automation and developer utilities.

I’m especially interested in taking an annoying or missing little thing and turning it into a working tool. Some projects start small, some grow into full systems, and the process is always part of the learning.

Built from a phone

A large part of my development workflow happens on Android with Termux, Git, CLI tools and cloud deployments.
