👋 Who I am 🌍

🚀 I’m DEVZIKKY, a developer and builder based in Lagos, Nigeria 🇳🇬. DEVZIKKY TECH is where I bring together the things I experiment with: 🌐 websites, 🔌 APIs, 🤖 AI assistants, 📧 email tools, 🛰️ bots, ⚙️ automation, and 🧰 developer utilities.

💡 I enjoy taking an annoying or missing little thing and turning it into a working tool. 🛠️ Some projects start small 🌱, while others grow into full systems 🏗️. Every build is another opportunity to experiment 🧪, solve problems 🧩, and learn something new 🧠✨.

📱 Built from a phone ⚡

💻 A large part of my development workflow happens on my system using 📲 Termux, 🌿 Git, ⌨️ CLI tools, and ☁️ cloud deployments. From writing code to launching projects 🚀, I enjoy turning ideas into working software. 🔥
